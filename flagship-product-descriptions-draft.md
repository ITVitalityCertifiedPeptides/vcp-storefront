## Tirzepatide

**Mechanism of action:** A 39-amino-acid synthetic peptide that acts as a
dual agonist at the glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and
glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptors, two G-protein-coupled receptors
implicated in incretin signaling and glucose homeostasis.

**Structure & identity:**
- Molecular formula: C225H348N48O68
- Molecular weight: ≈4813.5 g/mol
- CAS: 2023788-19-2
- Synonyms: LY3298176

**Research findings summary:** Published preclinical and clinical research
has studied tirzepatide's effects on glycemic parameters and body weight in
model systems and trial populations, with mechanistic work focused on its
dual-incretin receptor activity relative to single-agonist comparators.

**Purity/storage:** Supplied as a lyophilized powder, ≥98% purity by HPLC
(verify against lot COA). Store at -20°C, protected from light; reconstitute
with bacteriostatic water for laboratory preparation only.

## Retatrutide

**Mechanism of action:** A synthetic peptide conjugated to a lipid diacid
(a "triple agonist"), studied for activity at the GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon
receptors — a broader receptor-engagement profile than dual-agonist
compounds in the same research category.

**Structure & identity:**
- Molecular formula: C221H342N46O68
- Molecular weight: ≈4731.3 g/mol
- CAS: 2381089-83-2
- Synonyms: LY3437943

**Research findings summary:** Preclinical and clinical research literature
has studied retatrutide's effects on body weight and metabolic parameters
in the context of its triple-receptor mechanism, with comparative work
against dual-agonist and GLP-1-only compounds.

**Purity/storage:** Supplied as a lyophilized powder, ≥98% purity by HPLC
(verify against lot COA). Store at -20°C, protected from light; reconstitute
with bacteriostatic water for laboratory preparation only.

## CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin

*Note: Valhalla's SKU is explicitly "CJC-1295 (No DAC) / Ipamorelin" — the
DAC and no-DAC forms of CJC-1295 have different CAS numbers, molecular
formulas, and weights. The figures below distinguish the two; confirm which
is reflected on the actual supplier COA before publishing.*

**Mechanism of action:** A two-peptide combination studied together for
complementary activity on growth hormone release: CJC-1295 is a synthetic
growth-hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) analog, and Ipamorelin is a
selective growth-hormone secretagogue that acts at the ghrelin receptor.
Combined, the two are studied for their effect on pulsatile GH release
pathways acting through distinct receptor mechanisms.

**Structure & identity (CJC-1295, no-DAC / "Mod GRF 1-29"):**
- CAS: 863288-34-0 *(NOTE: some suppliers list this CAS against the DAC
  form instead — cross-check against Valhalla's COA, per the flag already
  raised in the Product Pricing & CAS Reference doc)*
- Molecular weight for the DAC-conjugated form is ≈3647.25 g/mol
  (formula C165H269N47O46); the no-DAC form is smaller since it lacks the
  albumin-binding DAC moiety — get exact figures from the lot COA.

**Structure & identity (Ipamorelin):**
- Molecular formula: C38H49N9O5
- Molecular weight: ≈711.85 g/mol
- CAS: 170851-70-4
- Sequence: Aib-His-D-2-Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH2 (pentapeptide)

**Research findings summary:** Published research has studied GHRH
analogs and ghrelin-receptor secretagogues, individually and in
combination, for their effects on growth hormone secretion patterns in
preclinical and early clinical models.

**Purity/storage:** Supplied as a lyophilized powder, ≥98% purity by HPLC
(verify against lot COA). Store at -20°C, protected from light; reconstitute
with bacteriostatic water for laboratory preparation only.

## KLOW (blend)

**Composition:** KLOW is a blend of four research peptides — GHK-Cu,
BPC-157, TB-500, and KPV — combined for research into overlapping tissue
and cellular-repair signaling pathways. No single CAS number applies to the
blend; each component has its own CAS (see individual compound pages/CSV
notes).

**Mechanism of action (by component):**
- GHK-Cu: a copper-binding tripeptide studied for effects on
  extracellular matrix remodeling and cellular signaling.
- BPC-157: a synthetic pentadecapeptide studied preclinically for effects
  on tissue repair pathways and angiogenesis signaling.
- TB-500: a synthetic fragment of thymosin beta-4, studied for effects on
  actin regulation and cellular migration pathways.
- KPV: a tripeptide fragment of alpha-MSH (11-13), studied preclinically
  for anti-inflammatory signaling activity.

**Research findings summary:** Published preclinical literature on each
component peptide individually has explored tissue-repair, anti-
inflammatory, and cell-migration signaling pathways; research on the
specific combined blend as sold is more limited than on the individual
compounds.

**Purity/storage:** Supplied as a lyophilized powder blend (verify exact
purity spec per component against lot COA). Store at -20°C, protected from
light; reconstitute with bacteriostatic water for laboratory preparation
only.

## BPC-157

**Mechanism of action:** A synthetic 15-amino-acid peptide derived from a
partial sequence of body protection compound (BPC), a peptide originally
identified in gastric juice. Studied preclinically for cytoprotective,
angiogenesis-related, and tissue-repair signaling activity.

**Structure & identity:**
- Molecular formula: C62H98N16O22
- Molecular weight: ≈1419.56 g/mol
- CAS: 137525-51-0
- Sequence: Gly-Glu-Pro-Pro-Pro-Gly-Lys-Pro-Ala-Asp-Asp-Ala-Gly-Leu-Val

**Research findings summary:** Preclinical literature has studied BPC-157
in animal models for its effects on tendon, ligament, and gastrointestinal
tissue signaling pathways, and for angiogenesis-related activity. Human
clinical data in the published literature remains limited.

**Purity/storage:** Supplied as a lyophilized powder, ≥98% purity by HPLC
(verify against lot COA). Store at -20°C, protected from light; reconstitute
with bacteriostatic water for laboratory preparation only.

## Bacteriostatic Water

**Composition:** Sterile water for injection containing 0.9% benzyl
alcohol as a bacteriostatic preservative, used in laboratory settings to
reconstitute lyophilized research peptides.

**Research use note:** Not itself a research compound under study — it is
a laboratory reagent/diluent. Included in the catalog because reconstituting
lyophilized peptides for laboratory use requires a compatible sterile
diluent.

**Purity/storage:** Store at room temperature, away from direct light, per
standard laboratory reagent handling. Once opened, follow standard aseptic
handling practices for the container's remaining shelf life (verify exact
shelf-life guidance against the specific product's own label/COA).
